# Financial Planning OS

A browser tool for people who hold private deals alongside everything else, and want to know
when the money arrives and whether it is enough to retire on.

**Everything runs in your browser.** Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and it works with
the wifi off. Close the tab and it forgets you were here.

## What it does

- **Private Deal Dashboard** — what you hold, and what the model makes of it
- **Liquidity Outlook** — when the money comes back, and what you still owe in capital calls
- **Retirement Simulator** — an accumulation and decumulation model over thousands of paths,
  with historical sampling drawn from 1928 to 2025
- **Performance & Returns** — how much comes back, and the range around it
- **Glossary & Background** — the assumptions, and what the tool cannot tell you
- **Loaded GC Deals** — the deals it already knows, and the names each one answers to

## The example book

The demo runs on a real book of Growth Circle deals, with the terms those deals actually carry,
so a member can check what they are looking at against their own statements rather than against
invented names. Load your own spreadsheet and it replaces all of it.

**Your tracker always wins.** A shared deal file only fills in what you left blank. If your copy
of the terms differs from the shared one, yours is used and nothing argues with you.

## Not advice

The probability assumptions are one investor's and are meant to be argued with. Anything you
type beats anything in a shared file.
